1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & MAJOR FUNCTIONAL MODULES
================================================================================
Between November 22 and November 23, 2026, RentTrack underwent comprehensive
architectural modernization, UI refinement, and backend logic enhancements across
both Admin and Tenant systems:

A. Tenant Portal Re-Architecture:
   - Decomposed monolithic tab view into dedicated Next.js App Router subroutes:
     * /tenant/overview
     * /tenant/my_room
     * /tenant/bill_and_payment
     * /tenant/profile
   - Removed the legacy tenant sidebar and collapse toggle; replaced with clean,
     persistent top header navigation buttons (Overview, My Room, Bills & Payments).
   - Designed a new non-slop Tenant Profile interface with active tab indicators
     and seamless back-navigation.
   - House Rules & Regulations logic: hardcoded policies, completely hidden during
     room selection ("Choose Room" view), and visible exclusively after room assignment.

B. Automated Billing & Utility Management:
   - Automated monthly billing cycle generating statements 7 days prior to due date
     (due on the 5th of each month, generated around the 28th/29th).
   - Standardized fixed utility rates across the system: Water (₱300/mo) and
     Electricity (₱700/mo). Removed obsolete submeter breakdown tables.
   - Dynamic Custom Charges: Admins can add itemized custom charges (e.g., room
     damages, key replacements) that reflect directly on the tenant's statement.
   - Automated Bill Rent Sync: When a tenant is reassigned to another room with a
     different rent rate, unsettled bills automatically sync to the new rent amount.
   - Official Receipt Download: Replaced generic payment view with official receipt
     view modal and Word (.docx) document generator.
   - Cleaned up obsolete "Pending Verification" KPI cards and filters; payments
     via E-Wallet (QR Ph) and Cash are recorded directly.

C. Email Notification Dispatch:
   - Integrated automated email notification service via Nodemailer with Gmail SMTP.
   - Whenever an invoice is generated (manually by admin or via cron/room selection),
     an official billing notification is sent to the tenant's sign-up Gmail account.

D. Room & Floor Management:
   - Renamed sidebar navigation item from "Rooms" to "Manage Rooms".
   - Consecutive Floor Validation: Enforced strict floor sequence rule (e.g., if
     floors 1-2 exist, entering floor 5 triggers a warning alert and backend block;
     only the next consecutive floor is permitted).
   - Permanent Floor Cleanup: Completely eliminated floors 4, 5, and 7 from the
     database and UI.
   - Whole Room Constraint: If rental type is "Whole Room", capacity is locked to 1
     bed and disabled from manual editing.
   - Cleaned Room Schema: Completely dropped legacy room "type" and "description" /
     "notes" fields from frontend, backend, and database schema.
   - Room Number Validation: Restricted room number inputs to positive integers only.

E. Room Matrix (Admin Dashboard):
   - Removed the room type subtitle ("Single • Whole Room", etc.) from every card.
   - Added Male and Female icons only: Lucide Mars (♂) in blue and Venus (♀) in rose
     aligned on the top header row opposite the room number, with no text clutter.

F. Tenant Management & Dossier:
   - Unassigned Profile Fix: Fixed the issue where unassigned tenants (e.g., Melvar
     Natividad) displayed "Room 101" on the right-hand profile panel. Unassigned
     residents now properly display "No Room" and "Unassigned".
   - Split Name Inputs: Replaced single "Full Name" input with separate "First Name"
     and "Last Name" fields in Add Tenant dialog.
   - Pending Account Actions: For pending accounts, actions dropdown restricted strictly
     to "Approve Account" and "Reject Account". Full management options display only
     once approved.
   - Emergency Contact: Added "Contact #" label below relation; removed telephone icon.
   - Removed Tenant ID metrics and badges from view modal and cards.

2.2. BILLING, PAYMENTS & UTILITY CHARGES SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Standardized Fixed Utility Charges:
  * backend/utils/billingUtils.js enforces FIXED_WATER_CHARGE = 300 and
    FIXED_ELECTRICITY_CHARGE = 700.
  * Removed legacy submeter inputs and breakdown tables across all tenant and admin views.
- Dynamic Additional Charges:
  * Added `additional_charges` JSONB column to `bills` table.
  * Admins can append custom line-item charges (damages, key replacement, penalties)
    which display dynamically in the tenant's active statement.
- 7-Day Advance Billing Generation:
  * Automatic billing cron (`billingCron.js`, `billingUtils.js`) generates monthly
    statements 7 days before the monthly due date (5th of each month).
- Automatic Rent Sync on Room Transfer:
  * Implemented `syncTenantBillsWithNewRoom` in backend/utils/billingUtils.js.
  * When a tenant moves to a room with a different rent price, active/unsettled bills
    automatically update to the new room's monthly rate.
- Official Receipt Generation & DOCX Download:
  * Created frontend/src/lib/official-receipt-docx.ts using the `docx` library.
  * "View Receipt" button in Payment History opens the official receipt and provides
    instant .docx download with official RentTrack formatting.
- Removal of Pending Verification:
  * E-Wallet and cash transactions record directly as verified; removed obsolete
    "Pending Verification" dropdown filter, modal flows, and KPI metrics.
  * Shifted billing KPI metrics from yearly to monthly calculation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3. AUTOMATED EMAIL NOTIFICATION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Gmail SMTP Integration:
  * In backend/services/emailService.js, implemented `sendBillGeneratedEmail`.
  * Supports direct Gmail SMTP (`smtp.gmail.com`, port 465/587) with standard Google
    App Passwords (`SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS` in backend/.env).
  * Automatically handles fallback to Ethereal/mock transport in development.
- Automatic Bill Generation Email Dispatch:
  * Integrated email trigger in `createBill` (backend/controllers/billingController.js),
    `autoGenerateBills`, and `autoGenerateBillsForTenant` (backend/utils/billingUtils.js).
  * Automatically fetches the tenant's sign-up email (`users.email`) and dispatches
    a branded HTML billing notice with room details, itemized breakdown, due date,
    and a one-click CTA button to the Tenant Portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4. ROOM & BED MANAGEMENT (MANAGE ROOMS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Navigation & Sidebar:
  * Renamed sidebar navigation item from "Rooms" to "Manage Rooms" in Admin layout.
- Schema & Input Simplification:
  * Removed legacy `type` and `description` / `notes` fields from rooms table,
    adminController, and frontend forms.
  * Enforced integer-only room numbers (`^[0-9]+$`).
- Whole Room Capacity Constraint:
  * If "Whole Room" is selected, bed capacity is locked to 1 and the capacity input
    is disabled from manual editing.
- Consecutive Floor Validation:
  * Implemented `validateConsecutiveFloor` in backend/controllers/adminController.js.
  * If floors 1 and 2 exist, entering floor 5 triggers a 400 error and an in-modal
    amber warning alert; only floor 3 is accepted next.
- Permanent Cleanup of Floors 4, 5, 7:
  * Removed all rooms belonging to floors 4, 5, and 7; cleaned references in billing,
    requests, and metrics across all controllers and UI matrix files.
- Single-Row Header Alignment:
  * In the room management sidebar, aligned Room Number, Gender Badge, Status Badge,
    and Close button on a single row with text-only styling.

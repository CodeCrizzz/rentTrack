================================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
RentTrack is an all-in-one property management web platform built specifically 
for boarding houses, dormitories, and multi-unit residential properties. 
The system bridges the operational gap between property administrators (landlords/
property managers) and residents (tenants/boarders) by digitizing and automating:
  - Room inventory and occupancy tracking
  - Recurring monthly billing cycles and utility breakdown
  - Payment collection, proof-of-payment review, and financial reconciliation
  - Tenant maintenance and repair ticketing
  - Property-wide announcements and direct messaging
  - Simulated CCTV / premises security monitoring
  - Rich real-time analytical dashboards for business health

================================================================================
2. STAKEHOLDERS & USER ROLES
================================================================================
The system enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

[1] PROPERTY ADMINISTRATOR (Landlord / Manager)
    - Full operational authority over property data, financial records, tenant
      directory, room inventory, maintenance requests, and notice broadcasts.
    - Access to advanced analytical KPIs, revenue metrics, and cash flow reports.

[2] RESIDENT / TENANT (Boarder / Renter)
    - Self-service portal to view tenancy details, select/apply for rooms, view
      itemized monthly invoices, submit proof of payments, log maintenance tickets,
      chat with the landlord, and read property announcements.

3. FUNCTIONAL SCOPE BY MODULE
================================================================================

--- 3.1 AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS CONTROL ---
- User Registration & Account Creation (Sign up with personal and contact details).
- Secure JWT-based Authentication (Token authorization with expiration).
- Password Hashing & Encryption (bcryptjs).
- Role verification middleware separating Admin routes (/admin/*) from 
  Tenant routes (/tenant/*).
- Dynamic session persistence and route protection.

--- 3.2 ADMIN DASHBOARD & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE ---
- Real-time KPI Metric Cards:
  * Total Rooms, Occupied Units, Available Units, Units under Maintenance.
  * Total Tenants, Active Tenants, Pending Assignment, Inactive Tenants.
  * Monthly Income, Pending Dues, Overdue Receivables, Revenue Trends.
- Financial Analytics & Charts (Powered by Recharts):
  * Cash flow trends comparing billed vs. collected revenue.
  * Historical revenue tracking and monthly revenue breakdown.
  * Collection efficiency and delinquency rates.
- Operational Watchlists:
  * Pending tenant approvals and room allocations.
  * Overdue accounts requiring collection follow-up (high priority for flexible stays).
  * Pending payment verifications awaiting review.
  * Live feed of recent activities, new messages, and open repair requests.

--- 3.3 ROOM & INVENTORY MANAGEMENT ---
- Unit Inventory Overview:
  * Room numbers, unit types, capacity, current occupants, and floor levels.
  * Pricing configuration: Monthly base rental rates per room or per bedspace.
  * Amenity tagging (e.g., Air conditioning, private bath, Wi-Fi, balcony).
- Status Lifecycle Management:
  * "Available" (Vacant)
  * "Partially Occupied" (Dormitory/bedspace sharing mode)
  * "Occupied" (At full capacity)
  * "Maintenance" (Temporarily offline for repairs)
- CRUD Operations: Create new rooms, update unit specs/pricing, delete units.
- Direct tenant assignment to specific rooms.

--- 3.4 TENANT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT ---
- Directory of all registered tenants with detailed profiles:
  * Full name, email address, phone number, and emergency contact details.
  * Assigned room number and occupancy status.
  * Flexible tenancy model: Open-ended stay, tenants can leave anytime (no fixed lease contracts). Move-in date and optional expected departure.
- Status Lifecycle:
  * "Pending Room Assignment" (New signups awaiting placement or approval)
  * "Active" (Currently residing in an assigned room)
  * "Inactive" (Moved out or suspended)
- Tenant profile modification, room reassignment, and account termination.

--- 3.5 AUTOMATED BILLING & INVOICING ENGINE ---
- Automated Recurring Billing:
  * Scheduled background cron engine (node-cron) executing monthly generation.
  * Automatic generation of itemized bills for all active residents.
- Itemized Cost Breakdown:
  * Base Rent amount
  * Water charges
  * Electricity charges
  * Ancillary / miscellaneous fees
  * Total amount calculated dynamically
- Billing Lifecycle & Status Tracking:
  * "Unpaid" (New invoice generated, awaiting payment)
  * "Partial" (Partial payment verified, remaining balance tracked)
  * "Paid" (Balance cleared)
  * "Overdue" (Due date lapsed without settlement)
- Administrative manual bill creation, modification, balance recalculation, and deletion.

--- 3.6 PAYMENTS & FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION ---
- Tenant Payment Submission:
  * Support for modern payment channels (GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, Cash).
  * Transaction reference number submission.
  * Proof-of-payment receipt upload (JPEG/PNG/PDF via Multer).
- Payment Verification Workflow:
  * "Pending Verification": Uploaded receipts queued for admin validation.
  * Admin Receipt Inspector: Image preview, verification of amount & reference.
  * Approval Action: Automatically updates bill balance and marks as Paid/Partial.
  * Rejection Action: Leaves bill unpaid and notifies tenant.
- Payment History & Audit Ledger:
  * Comprehensive payment logs with timestamps, methods, and receipt records.

--- 3.7 MAINTENANCE & REPAIR TICKETING ---
- Tenant Request Submission:
  * Category classification (Plumbing, Electrical, Carpentry, Appliance, Internet, General).
  * Priority rating (Low, Medium, High, Emergency).
  * Issue description and photo/attachment upload.
- Request Status Lifecycle:
  * "Pending" -> "In Progress" -> "Completed" -> "Cancelled"
- Admin Resolution Desk:
  * Update ticket progress, assign technician status, mark resolved.
  * Tenant cancellation ability for pending tickets.

--- 3.8 PREMISES CCTV & SECURITY MONITORING ---
- Multi-channel Virtual Video Monitoring Matrix:
  * 12 distinct surveillance feeds (Main Gate, Lobby, Parking, Hallways, Rooftop,
    Basement, Elevators, Cafeteria, Emergency Exits, Loading Dock).
- Interactive Grid View Switcher (2-column, 3-column, or 4-column layout).
- Fullscreen Camera Inspection modal with simulated live timecode, FPS, and resolution.
- Live camera status indicators (Online, Offline, Signal Degradation).

--- 3.9 INTERNAL MESSAGING & CHAT ---
- Secure one-to-one messaging channel between Tenants and Administrators.
- Conversation indexing, message timestamps, and unread notification badges.
- Tenant quick-inquiry and issue clarification platform.

--- 3.10 ANNOUNCEMENTS & DIGITAL BULLETIN BOARD ---
- Administrative notice broadcasting:
  * Priority flags (Standard, Important, Urgent).
  * Target audience segmentation (All Tenants, Specific Blocks/Floors).
- Real-time display on Tenant Dashboard and dedicated Announcement feed.

--- 3.11 TENANT SELF-SERVICE PORTAL ---
- Tenant Dashboard:
  * Live animated balance counter with currency formatting.
  * Current room details and occupancy summary.
  * Recent payment transactions and upcoming due date alert.
  * Quick links to Pay Rent, Submit Request, and Message Admin.
- Room Selection Module:
  * Browse available rooms with photos, amenities, and rate details.
  * Request/claim an available room upon initial registration.
- Profile & Security:
  * Manage contact info, phone, and emergency contact details.
  * Change password with secure validation.

4. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & STACK
================================================================================

--- 4.1 FRONTEND ---
- Framework        : Next.js 15 (React 19, TypeScript, App Router architecture)
- Styling          : Tailwind CSS v4, Custom CSS Design System
- Animations       : Framer Motion (page transitions, micro-animations, counters)
- Visualizations   : Recharts (Composed charts, bar charts, area charts)
- Theme Engine     : next-themes (Seamless Dark Mode and Light Mode support)
- Icons            : Lucide React
- Networking       : Axios with centralized API client and bearer token interception
- PWA Readiness    : Service Worker (sw.ts) and Web App Manifest (manifest.ts)

--- 4.2 BACKEND API ---
- Runtime & Server : Node.js with Express.js v5 RESTful API
- Database Driver  : pg (node-postgres connection pool with explicit schema)
- Database         : PostgreSQL (rentTrack_db, rentTrack_schema)
- Authentication   : jsonwebtoken (JWT) + bcryptjs
- File Uploads     : Multer disk storage (/uploads/payments, /uploads/requests)
- Scheduled Tasks  : node-cron (Automated monthly billing job)
- Security         : CORS, Route protection middleware, role guard middleware

--- 4.3 DATABASE SCHEMA ENTITIES ---
- users         : Tenant and admin credentials, profiles, status, assigned room_id
- rooms         : Room inventory, occupancy numbers, rate, amenities, status
- bills         : Invoices, breakdown (rent/utilities/fees), balance, due date, status
- payments      : Payment records, proof image paths, reference IDs, verification status
- requests      : Maintenance requests, issue category, priority, attachments, status
- messages      : Direct communications between tenants and admins, read status
- announcements : Broadcast announcements, priority, creation timestamp

5. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS & QUALITY ATTRIBUTES
================================================================================
- Visual Excellence & Aesthetics: High-end UI design with glassmorphic cards,
  dark-mode optimization, fluid transitions, and clear data typography.
- Mobile Responsiveness: Adaptable layouts optimized across smartphones, tablets,
  and desktop displays.
- Data Consistency & Security: Passwords stored as irreversible hashes; all
  financial transactions linked to explicit bill IDs and audit timestamps.
- Resilience & Offline Capability: PWA manifest allows app installation on mobile 
  homescreens; graceful error handling on API endpoints.

6. CURRENT BOUNDARIES & OUT-OF-SCOPE ITEMS
================================================================================
The following items are outside the current release scope but serve as candidates
for future roadmap iterations:
- Direct Automated Payment Gateway Webhooks (e.g., Stripe, PayMongo, Xendit API);
  current scope uses user-submitted receipt verification.
- SMS / Twilio Gateway Notifications (current notifications are in-app).
- Physical Smart Lock / IoT Hardware Integration for keyless room entry.
- Multi-property / Multi-landlord SaaS Tenancy (current deployment is single-property).
- Automated Utility Meter OCR or IoT Sub-metering for automatic water/electric reading.
